03 · Non-negotiable Scheduling Rules

Purchase steel early

Steel purchasing follows BM coordination, not engineering. Order only verified sections and quantities; confirm supplier lead time.

10 days for steel fabrication

Allow 10 full working days for fabrication and finishing, followed by 2 days for QA. Start only after sign-off and material receipt.

D30 = installation start

Installation starts only after QA, materials, delivery and site / installer readiness. Builders must confirm access and workfront.
